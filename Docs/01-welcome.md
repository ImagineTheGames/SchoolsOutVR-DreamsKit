# Welcome to the Dreams Creator Kit

A Dream is a short game that you build inside School's Out VR. It has its own map, its own rules and its own
clock. Players pick it from the Play Games screen, bring their Crew, and play it just like Prank Night.

This guide shows you how to build one, test it, and send it in.

## What you need

- A Windows PC.
- Unreal Engine 5.8 from the Epic Games launcher. Use the newest 5.8 version the launcher offers.
- About 20 GB of free disk space.
- A free mod.io account. This is where your Dream gets its page.
- A headset is nice to have for testing, but you do not need one to start.

## What the Kit is

The Kit is a small Unreal project. You open it in the normal Unreal Engine from the launcher. It does not
contain the game and it does not replace your engine.

It has three things:

- The **Dream Palette**. These are the ready-made pieces from the game: doors, items, devices, score zones
  and more. You place them in your map like any other actor.
- The **Dreams panel**. This is where you make a new Dream, check it, and send it in.
- A **Sandbox** map to start from, so you never begin with an empty room.

## What the Kit is not

The pieces in the Kit are stand-ins. They look right and they behave close to the real thing, so you can
build and test your layout and your own Blueprints in the editor. Some effects need the real game, and
the Kit only shows a placeholder for those. For example, the janitor stands still in the editor and a
splat is a simple marker.

The real behaviour shows up when your Dream runs in the game. You will see it there after you submit.

## How it works, start to finish

1. **Build.** Open the Kit in Unreal Engine 5.8. Use the New Dream button in the Dreams panel. Place
   pieces from the Palette and build your map.
2. **Test in the editor.** Press Play. Walk around, grab things, check that the round starts and ends the
   way you want.
3. **Check.** Press Validate. The Kit tells you what to fix and points at the exact asset.
4. **Submit.** Press Submit. The Kit sends your Dream to the Dreams Forge.
5. **We build it for you.** The Dreams Forge makes the three game files: Windows, Quest and Steam Frame.
   You never have to cook, package or set up Android. You only send your project.
6. **Watch the status.** The Dreams panel shows where your Dream is. When it is ready, one button opens
   your Dream's page on mod.io.

A Dream goes live only when all three files are ready, so players on every device can play it together.

## How to read this guide

Each page is short. Start here, then read the pages in order the first time. After that, use them as
a checklist.

- Match setup: how long a round lasts, how many players, how it ends.
- Performance on Quest: the limits that keep your Dream smooth.
- Building for a VR body: sizes and heights that feel right in a headset.
- Dreams and Nightmares: what content is welcome.
- mod.io: your page, your tags, and what happens after you submit.
- Troubleshooting: the messages you might see, and what to do.
- Contest: where the rules live.
