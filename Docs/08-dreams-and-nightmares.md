# Dreams and Nightmares

Every Dream has a Realm. The Realm is a label that tells players what to expect before they play.

- **Dream.** A friendly, playful map. Think school hallways, games and pranks.
- **Nightmare.** A spooky map. Darkness, chases and creepy characters are all fine.

You pick the Realm in the match settings. It shows as a badge on your card and as a tag on mod.io.

## Who plays

School's Out VR is rated for teens (13 and up). Everything in your Dream should be fine for that audience,
even in a Nightmare. Scary is welcome. Cruel is not.

## Allowed and not allowed

| Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Darkness, shadows and fog | Blood, gore or dismemberment |
| Chases and jump scares | Torture or cruelty |
| Creepy janitors and strange monsters | Sexual content |
| Spooky sounds and music you made or are licensed to use | Drugs, alcohol or tobacco |
| Pranks and silly props | Realistic firearms or real weapons |
| Cartoon-style slapstick | Self-harm |
| Your own original characters | Hate symbols |
| Invented names and brands | Real people or real brands |
| Your own music and sounds | Characters, music or sounds from someone else's work |

## Also required

- **No fast flashing.** Lights and materials must not blink faster than 3 times per second.
- **Keep it playable.** A Nightmare can be dark, but players must be able to find their way.
- **Clean language.** Names, signs and text go through a strict language filter.
- **No exploits.** Do not build ways to get free money, leave the map, or get unlimited items.

## What happens if there is a problem

A reviewer plays every Dream before it goes live. If something does not fit, we send it back with a short
reason in the mod.io comments. You can fix it and submit again.

Remember: you own your Dream. We only ask that it is safe and fun for the players who pick it.
