# Building for a VR body

In VR the player is the camera, the body and the hands. A room that looks fine on a monitor can be
uncomfortable or impossible in a headset. This page is a short list of what to design for.

## The player's size

- **Eyes are the same for everyone.** The game sets every player's eye height to 170 cm.
- **Arms are not.** Reach is different for every player. A shelf that is easy for one player is a stretch for
  another.
- **The body is about 40 cm wide and 192 cm tall.** Design for the larger body so nobody gets stuck.

## Doorways, halls and ceilings

- A gap that looks wide enough may not fit the body. Test it by walking through in Play.
- We suggest halls at least 100 cm wide and ceilings at least 220 cm high where players walk.
- Measure the real opening. Posts, rails, signs and chair backs make a gap smaller than it looks.
- Leave room to stop and turn at the end of every stair and ramp.
- Do not make a path that needs squeezing. Do not make the player crouch unless it is part of the fun,
  and never in the way of the only route.

## Things players reach for

- **Put things a little high, not low.** A tall player can use a high shelf easily. A short player cannot
  bend down all day. Bending is hard in a headset.
- **Buttons, levers, hooks and shelves belong 60 to 150 cm above the floor where the player stands.**
  Anything outside this range gets a warning.
- Leave a clear spot to stand in front of every control. Players need a path for their body and a path for
  their hand.
- Make labels clear. A funny picture should never hide what a button does.

## Climbing

- The first grip should be no higher than 200 cm.
- A ladder must end on a landing. The player has to lift their feet over the edge, so give them grips above
  the landing and room to step off.
- Leave space to land after letting go.

## Both hands

Test every grab and every hold with both the left hand and the right hand. A thing that feels fine in the
right hand may be flipped in the left. A reviewer checks both.

## Seated players

Many players sit. Make sure they can finish your Dream seated.

- Never require something below knee height.
- Never require something behind the player.
- Do not move the camera for the player.
- Do not change the player's size.
- Keep moving platforms slow, under 300 cm per second.

## Comfort

- When you teleport a player, use the fade pad from the Palette so the screen fades first.
- Do not flash lights or materials faster than 3 times per second.
- Keep dark maps playable. Nightmares can be dark, but players still need to see where to go.

## Final check

Numbers help, but only a headset tells you how it feels. If you have one, try your Dream seated and standing
before you submit. If you do not, ask a friend who does.
