# mod.io

mod.io is where players find, download and rate your Dream. Each Dream has one page on mod.io. The Kit
helps you set it up.

## Your account

1. Make a free account at mod.io if you do not have one.
2. In the Dreams panel, press Sign in and type the email you use on mod.io.
3. mod.io emails you a short code. Type it into the panel.

That is all. The Kit never sees your password. It keeps a sign-in token on your own PC, outside your project,
so it can never be sent by mistake. If it expires, the panel asks you to sign in again.

You can undo it any time from your mod.io account.

## Your first Submit

The first time you press Submit, the Kit does not yet know which mod.io page is yours.

1. The Kit opens the mod.io page for adding a new mod.
2. Fill in the name, a short summary, a picture, and your tags. Save the page.
3. Copy the web address of your new page.
4. Go back to the Kit and paste the link when it asks.

The Kit checks that the page really is yours. Then it sends your Dream. You only do this once per Dream.

## Tags

Set these on your mod.io page:

- **Realm.** Dream or Nightmare. This should match your match settings.
- **Players.** How many players your Dream is for.
- **Comfort.** How comfortable it is in VR.
- **Dream Type.** What kind of game it is.

## What happens after you press Submit

1. The Dreams Forge checks your Dream.
2. If something is wrong, the panel shows what and where. Fix it and press Submit again. Nothing goes to
   mod.io until it passes.
3. If it passes, the Forge builds the Windows, Quest and Steam Frame files and uploads them to your page.
4. A reviewer plays your Dream and either approves it or sends it back with a reason.
5. When all three files are approved, your Dream is live.

The Dreams panel shows the status at every step. When it is ready, press **Open my Dream on mod.io** to see
your page.

## Versions and updates

- Each time you Submit, you make a new version.
- When School's Out VR updates, we rebuild every Dream for the new game version. You do not have to do
  anything.
- If a Dream stops passing our checks after an update, we may take it off the list until it is fixed. We will
  tell you.

## Rules that apply

Your page follows the mod.io Terms of Use and Acceptable Use Policy. They apply as soon as you create the
page, and they are mod.io's rules, not ours.
